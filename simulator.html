Why the Screw Moves the Needle Most:
Because the button sits ~253 mm behind the balance point, every +1.0 g added to the screw pulls the balance point roughly 3.8 mm closer to the hand. By contrast, bare stick weight is distributed across both sides of the fulcrum, so changing stick weight alone barely shifts the balance point unless fittings change with it.

Note: Typical prediction uncertainty is ±4.4 mm due to natural Pernambuco density variations along individual sticks.
